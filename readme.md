### About

I'm Kyle, **ImKyleJK** online. A self-taught, self-employed developer in Scotland: full-stack apps in Node.js, Svelte and Next.js, interface and logo design, and the Linux infrastructure that keeps it all running.

I like small projects that teach me how something actually works, shipped quickly rather than planned to death.

### Right now

- **Building** [Rotaaa](https://rotaaa.app), free and open-source rota planning for small teams
- **Running** [Network Camp](https://network.camp), network diagnostics with no ads and no sign-up
- **Studying** at UHI, heading into a part-time BSc in Applied Software Development
- **Contributing** to open source with the [Contributors Club](https://contrib.club) since 2025
- **Writing** up what I learn on [the blog](https://imkylejk.me/blog)
